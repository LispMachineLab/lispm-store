## Your agents at work

LispM Office adds a pixel office to LispM Agent's **Work** view. Choose a project in the Agents list to see its working agents together, or switch to all projects. Each room has its own floor, walls and furnishings, and each agent has a pixel avatar.

Working agents type at their desks. Idle agents take a coffee break, agents waiting for help raise a hand, and agents asking a colleague move beside them. Select an agent to inspect its current work inside the same view, then return to the room or open its conversation.

## Part of your workspace

Office is an optional LispM Agent extension. It works in ordinary Emacs as well as LispM Desktop; it does not start another application or create another agent. Its rooms reflect the sessions LispM Agent already knows about.

The LispM distribution installs Office by default. **Uninstall** removes its Work renderer and closes its views; your agents and conversations remain available. It stays removed after restarting LispM. Choose **Get** here to install it again.

Office requires LispM Agent 0.15.0 and LispM UI 0.6.0 or later. LispM Agent remains usable without Office.
